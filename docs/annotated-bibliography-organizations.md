Chen, Y., Ramamurthy, K., & Wen, K.-W. (2012). Organizations' information security policy compliance: Stick or Carrot Approach? Journal of Management Information Systems, 29(3), 157–188. http://doi.org/10.2753/mis0742-1222290305.

Employee error and internal breaches are a far greater threat to infosec companies today. To deal with those problems, this study creates a compliance theory and generate a research model between coercive control, which has been advocated by scholars and widely practiced by companies; remunerative control, for research and practical situations, which usually is not implemented by the companies. A web-field experiment involving real situations with employees in their usual settings was used to empirically test a model. While providing further support for general deterrence theory, our findings imply that mechanisms in IS security, through better control of rewards, could be an alternative for all organizations that have problems preventing breaches. Other points are very interesting is the significance of interactions between punishment and reward found in the study further indicates a need for a more comprehensive enforcement system that includes reward scheme through which the organization ethical standards and values are more established or reemphasized by the studies.
