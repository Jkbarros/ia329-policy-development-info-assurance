Policy Plan: 1st Draft.

For your first draft, you will be identifying the pillars or "branches" of your policy.

Each pillar/branch will identify a sub-section of your policy. For example: an email policy would have "deleted emails" as a sub-section.

I encourage you to do a little bit of research on your chosen policy type to come up with your policy branches. You will not be writing your final paper on all of the branches, only 2 or 3 (depending on the scope).

You need to identify a minimum of 5 branches. I encourage you to go past the minimum as policies are often complex.
